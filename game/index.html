You're the newest analyst on the Forensic Technology team.
The data doesn't lie — but it does hide.

Your job: a live transaction feed from a client under investigation lands on your screen. For each transaction, decide fast — 🚩 FLAG it for the investigation team, or ✅ CLEAR it as legitimate business.

Flag real fraud to score points and build a streak multiplier. False-flag a legitimate payment and the client loses trust in the engagement. Miss real fraud and they lose even more. Trust hits zero? The engagement is over.

Each level briefs you on new fraud typologies — the same ones real forensic teams hunt for.

Keyboard: F or ← to flag  •  C or → to clear

A training game inspired by real forensic data-analytics work: structuring detection,
shell-entity screening, round-tripping and vendor fraud analytics. All data is synthetic.
